# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

This Privacy Policy describes how Thomas Stringham, as a sole proprietor based in British Columbia, Canada ("we," "us," "our"), collects, uses, and protects information from visitors and users of ainetscape.com (the "Service"). We comply with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy law in British Columbia.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

- **Generation briefs:** The text you enter into the AI Composer dialog
- **Generated content:** The HTML pages produced by AI Composer based on your briefs
- **Email addresses (optional):** If you submit a report via the Report Page form or contact us
- **Sharing preferences:** Whether you have marked a generated page as public or private

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Service we automatically collect:

- **IP address:** Used in hashed form for rate-limiting, vote-deduplication, and basic analytics. We do not store IP addresses in plaintext; we store SHA-256 hashes.
- **Cookies:** We set cookies for the following purposes:
 - `author-{pageId}` cookies identify you as the author of pages you have generated, so we can show you a privacy toggle on those pages
 - `voted-{pageId}` cookies prevent you from voting on the same page twice
 - Vercel Web Analytics may set cookies for aggregated traffic measurement
 - Google Analytics, if enabled, may set cookies for aggregated traffic measurement
- **Usage events:** We log events such as generation starts, completions, refusals, shares, votes, hits, and timeouts. Events are tied to hashed IPs and page IDs, not to your identity.
- **Browser metadata:** User-agent string and referrer URL, used for analytics.

### 1.3 What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

- Your real name (unless you voluntarily provide it via a report or contact email)
- Your physical address
- Payment information
- Account credentials (the Service does not have user accounts)
- Biometric or health information
- Information about other people you may include in your User Content (please do not include such information without their consent)

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide the Service (generate pages from your briefs, render pages, serve the Gallery)
- Operate basic security and rate-limiting (anti-abuse, anti-gaming of votes)
- Measure aggregate site usage to improve the Service
- Respond to reports and inquiries
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

- **With service providers** who operate infrastructure on our behalf (Vercel for hosting, MongoDB Atlas for data storage, Anthropic and xAI for AI generation, Google for analytics if enabled). These providers have their own privacy policies and are contractually limited in their use of your data to providing the Service.
- **For legal compliance** if required by law, court order, or regulatory request.
- **To protect rights and safety** if we believe disclosure is necessary to prevent harm to users or third parties.

### 3.1 Note on AI Generation

When you submit a brief to AI Composer, that brief is transmitted to a third-party AI provider (xAI primary, Anthropic Claude as fallback) for processing. The provider's privacy policy applies to their handling of your brief. We do not control the provider's data retention or use of submitted prompts for model training. Do not submit information you do not want potentially used by an AI provider.

## 4. Public Content

Pages you mark as public via the "Make site public" option appear in the AI Composer Gallery and are visible to all visitors. The original brief that produced a page is stored privately and never shown publicly. The generated HTML is publicly visible at the page's unique URL.

You may toggle a page's public/private status at any time using the author controls on the page (visible only to authors via the author cookie).

## 5. Data Retention

We retain:

- **Generated pages:** Indefinitely, unless deleted by the author or removed by us under our content policies
- **Hashed IPs and event logs:** Up to 12 months for security and analytics purposes
- **Author cookies:** 1 year from set date
- **Vote and hit records:** Indefinitely (for displaying historical vote and hit counts on pages)

You may request deletion of any page you authored by contacting webmaster@ainetscape.com with proof of authorship (the author cookie token).

## 6. Your Rights Under PIPEDA

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information
- **Withdraw consent** for our collection or use of your personal information (which may require us to discontinue your access to the Service)
- **Challenge our compliance** with PIPEDA principles

To exercise these rights, contact webmaster@ainetscape.com.

If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

## 7. International Users

The Service is operated from Canada. Data we collect may be transmitted to, stored, and processed in any country where we or our service providers maintain facilities (including the United States and other jurisdictions). By using the Service, you consent to such transfers.

If you are located in the European Union, please be aware that we are not currently designed for GDPR compliance. We do not knowingly target EU users, and we may decline to process requests from EU users until appropriate compliance infrastructure is in place.

## 8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact webmaster@ainetscape.com and we will delete such information.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption, hashed IP storage, and access controls on our database. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Material changes will be indicated by updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
