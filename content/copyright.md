# Copyright Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

This policy explains how AI Netscape responds to claims of copyright infringement. As a Canadian-operated service, we follow the notice-and-notice regime established under the Canadian Copyright Act. We also voluntarily respond to good-faith takedown requests from rights holders worldwide.

## 1. Filing a Copyright Notice

If you believe that content on AI Netscape infringes your copyright, please send a written notice to webmaster@ainetscape.com containing:

1. **Your identity:** Your full legal name and a means of contact (email is sufficient)
2. **Identification of the work:** The copyrighted work you claim has been infringed (title, registration number if applicable, link to the original work or other identification)
3. **Identification of the infringing material:** The URL of the page on AI Netscape you claim infringes (e.g., `https://ainetscape.com/p/abc123XYZ`)
4. **A statement** that you believe in good faith that the use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
5. **A statement** that the information in the notice is accurate
6. **Your signature** (electronic signature is acceptable)

False or bad-faith notices may result in legal liability for the sender.

## 2. Our Response

Upon receiving a complete and good-faith notice, we will:

1. Review the notice within a reasonable timeframe (typically 1-3 business days)
2. If the claim appears valid, remove or restrict access to the allegedly infringing content
3. Notify the page's author, if identifiable, that the content has been removed and the basis for removal

We may, at our discretion, decline to act on notices that appear incomplete, frivolous, or in bad faith.

## 3. Counter-Notices

If you are the author of a page that has been removed and you believe the removal was in error or that you have the right to use the material, you may send a counter-notice to webmaster@ainetscape.com containing:

1. Your full legal name and contact information
2. Identification of the page that was removed
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you believe the removal was the result of mistake or misidentification
4. Your consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of British Columbia, Canada
5. Your electronic signature

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice, we may restore the content at our discretion. We are not obligated to restore content based on a counter-notice.

## 4. Repeat Infringers

We may, in appropriate circumstances, restrict or terminate access to the Service by users who repeatedly infringe copyright.

## 5. AI-Generated Content

AI Netscape uses artificial intelligence models to generate HTML pages based on user briefs. Generated content may unintentionally resemble copyrighted works. Authors of pages on the Service are responsible for ensuring that their published content does not infringe third-party rights.

If you believe a generated page reproduces protected material without authorization, please file a notice as described above.

## 6. Note on DMCA

The Digital Millennium Copyright Act is United States law. AI Netscape is operated from Canada and is not subject to the DMCA's mandatory designated-agent registration requirements. However, we respond in good faith to copyright takedown requests received in DMCA format, treating them as notices under our Canadian notice-and-notice policy.

If you are a US rights holder, you may submit a DMCA-style notice to webmaster@ainetscape.com; we will treat it under this Copyright Policy.
